About Miami Academy

Building a foundation for success and lifelong learning.

Who We Are

Founded in 2022, Miami Academy is a family institution built on the belief that education should inspire, empower, and transform lives. From the very beginning, the academy has been dedicated to creating a warm and supportive environment where every learner feels valued, encouraged, and motivated to achieve their full potential. As a family centered school, we take pride in fostering strong relationships between students, teachers, and parents, creating a close knit community that promotes both academic excellence and personal growth. At Miami Academy, learning goes beyond the classroom as we nurture confidence, leadership, creativity, and integrity in every student. Guided by our motto, "Vision with Action," we are committed to preparing young people with the knowledge, skills, and mindset needed to succeed in a rapidly changing world. Choosing Miami Academy means becoming part of a community that believes in your future and is dedicated to helping you turn your ambitions into reality.

Mission

We groom a global citizen with the skills and mindset to thrive and take the world.

Vision

To be a leading centre of academic excellence that empowers learners with knowledge, discipline, and innovation, producing confident, responsible, and globally competitive graduates who positively impact their communities and the world.

Principal's Message

At Miami Academy, we believe every learner possesses unique potential waiting to be unlocked.

Our commitment is to provide a nurturing, disciplined and innovative learning environment where students develop academic excellence, character, leadership and global competence.

We prepare learners not only to succeed in examinations but to become confident citizens capable of making meaningful contributions to society.

We invite you to join our community as we continue shaping future leaders through Vision with Action.

Examinations & Curriculum

We offer internationally recognised and locally relevant education pathways.

ZIMSEC

The Zimbabwe School Examinations Council (ZIMSEC) curriculum provides a strong national academic foundation from Form 1 to Form 6, focusing on core subjects aligned with Zimbabwe’s education system.

Cambridge

The Cambridge curriculum offers internationally benchmarked education, preparing learners for global opportunities through rigorous assessment and internationally recognised qualifications.

Admissions

Miami Academy welcomes students from Form 1 to Form 6.

Admission Requirements

Form 1 Entry

  • Completed application form
  • Copy of birth certificate
  • Grade 7 results slip
  • Recent passport size photographs
  • Transfer letter (optional)

Form 2 - Form 4

  • Completed application form
  • Copy of birth certificate
  • Most recent school report
  • Transfer letter from previous school
  • Recent passport size photographs

Lower & Upper Six

  • Completed application form
  • Certified birth certificate / ID
  • O Level results certificate
  • Most recent school report
  • Recent passport size photographs
